# Code of Conduct

Opaline is a small project that wants to stay friendly. Anyone can take part, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person. Assume good intent.
- Keep discussion on topic and in the open, so others can learn from it.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## What we won't accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and in any space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and temporarily or permanently ban people who break these rules.
To report a problem, email **<add a contact address before publishing>** or contact a maintainer privately. Reports are handled
confidentially, and we will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.

This code of conduct is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
